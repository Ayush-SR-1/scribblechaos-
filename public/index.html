How to Play

1. Join or Create a Room: Customize your artist name and avatar, then click Play Public or create a private room for your friends.

2. Drawing Phase: When it's your turn to draw, choose 1 of 3 word prompts. Use your brushes and colors to draw the secret word on the canvas before time runs out!

3. Guessing Phase: When another player draws, type your guess into the live chat box. The faster you guess, the more points you score!

4. Progressive Hints: Blank underscores (_ _ _ _) will reveal random letters over time to help you guess.

5. Winning: After all rounds finish, the artist with the highest score wins 1st place on the victory podium!
